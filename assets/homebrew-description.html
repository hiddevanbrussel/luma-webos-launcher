A native launcher for LG webOS TVs, inspired by Apple TV. Fork of Luma by Denis Efremov.

Launch apps and inputs, organize your favorites, and browse your app library with remote or Magic Remote pointer controls. Customize backgrounds and input labels, and add weather for a city of your choice. Connect Home Assistant from a page on your phone while Luma is running. English, German, Spanish and Simplified Chinese are included.

Settings, System, Aerial screensaver can start WebOS Aerial Screensaver after idle. Install that app and press Apply. Root is required for that screensaver and for Luma's root features. The launcher itself runs without root.
